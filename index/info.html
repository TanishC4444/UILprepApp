Competition structure

Four stages. One clear path.

UIL groups high schools into conferences by enrollment. Students typically progress from local competition toward the state championship.

How advancement works

  1. InvitationalsOptional preseason meets for practice and early feedback.
  2. DistrictThe first official championship meet. Top finishers advance.
  3. RegionDistrict qualifiers compete against students from across the region.
  4. StateThe UIL State Academic Championships bring top qualifiers to Austin.
