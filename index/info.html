Alignment

How Competition Works

UIL places schools into conferences (1A–6A) by enrollment. Within your conference, you advance through four levels.

1
Invitationals Two optional pre-season meets. Good practice, no stakes.
2
District First official meet. Top finishers advance.
3
Region District winners from across your region compete. Top advance.
4
State UIL State Academic Championships in Austin.
