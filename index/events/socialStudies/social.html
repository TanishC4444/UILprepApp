Mastery plan

Skills to build before meet day.

  1. Use the current UIL outline as the backbone of every study session.
  2. Build breadth across history, government, economics, geography, and culture.
  3. Practice interpreting excerpts, maps, visuals, and other primary-source material.
  4. Refresh materials each year because the official outline changes annually.
