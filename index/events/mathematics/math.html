Contest overview

Broad knowledge beats narrow tricks.

The 50-question multiple-choice test spans grade-appropriate numeration, operations, fractions, decimals, percents, exponents, probability, statistics, number theory, simple interest, geometry, and algebra.

Fractions, decimals & percentsExponents & order of operationsProbability, statistics & number theoryGeometry, measurement & algebra
Scoring

Skip strategically.

Incorrect responses lose points while blank answers do not, so disciplined decision-making matters.

+5 for a correct response · −2 for an incorrect response · 0 for a skipped question.
Mastery plan

Skills to build before meet day.

  1. Strong non-calculator computation across integers, fractions, decimals, and percents.
  2. Broad topic coverage rather than over-specializing in one area.
  3. Time management across all 50 questions with a deliberate skip-and-return plan.
  4. Careful reading to avoid losing points on preventable interpretation errors.
