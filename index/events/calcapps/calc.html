Contest overview

Accuracy at calculator speed.

Contestants use a silent hand-held calculator with no external power to solve straightforward computations, geometry, and stated problems modeled after recent textbooks. The working pace is roughly 22 seconds per problem.

Arithmetic operationsRoots and powersGeometry formulasApplied word problems
Scoring

Every attempted answer matters.

The grading rule rewards speed, but careless entries can erase that advantage.

+5 correct · −4 incorrect, skipped, or illegible. Problems are graded only through the last attempted item.
Mastery plan

Skills to build before meet day.

  1. Fast, accurate calculator operation without looking away from the test.
  2. Mental estimation to catch keying mistakes before committing an answer.
  3. Order-of-operations fluency plus instant recall of roots, powers, and geometry formulas.
  4. Timed endurance at a pace near 22 seconds per problem.
