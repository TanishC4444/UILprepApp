Contest overview

Think clearly while the clock runs.

Contestants draw three topics, choose one, and receive three minutes to prepare. The speech is delivered without notes and may run up to five minutes.

Draw three topicsChoose one promptThree-minute preparationNotes-free delivery
Scoring

Judges rank the full performance.

Organization, delivery, poise, and effective development matter more than reciting isolated facts.

Contestants are ranked by judges. Going over five minutes results in a one-rank penalty.
Mastery plan

Skills to build before meet day.

  1. Use a repeatable introduction–body–conclusion structure under pressure.
  2. Generate examples and supporting points quickly from an unfamiliar prompt.
  3. Control voice, body language, pacing, and eye contact without notes.
  4. Practice stretching or trimming ideas to land inside the time window.
