Contest overview

Build the case. Defend the side.

Students select one of five annual topics, research both pro and con positions, choose a side, and deliver a polished memorized speech that uses evidence to persuade.

Annual current-issue topicsPro and con researchPersuasive argumentMemorized delivery
Scoring

Judges rank argument and delivery together.

A strong script still needs credible evidence, organization, presence, and controlled timing.

Contestants are ranked by judges. Going over six minutes results in a one-rank penalty.
Mastery plan

Skills to build before meet day.

  1. Research credible evidence and evaluate claims from both sides.
  2. Build a persuasive argument with a clear thesis, warrants, and documented support.
  3. Memorize reliably without flattening vocal expression or audience connection.
  4. Acknowledge the opposition while confidently defending one position.
