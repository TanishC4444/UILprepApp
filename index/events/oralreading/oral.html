Contest overview

Interpret—not merely recite.

Students read a published literary selection aloud. Grades 4–6 perform poetry; grades 7–9 alternate prose and poetry on the UIL yearly schedule.

Poetry for grades 4–6Rotating prose/poetry for 7–9Published literatureUp to six minutes
Scoring

Judges evaluate the performance.

The strongest readings communicate meaning through vocal choices while staying grounded in the text.

Contestants are ranked by judges. Going over six minutes results in a one-rank penalty.
Mastery plan

Skills to build before meet day.

  1. Select literature that suits the reader’s voice, age, and expressive range.
  2. Control pacing, breath, emphasis, articulation, and vocal variety.
  3. Make interpretive choices that clarify meaning without overacting.
  4. Rehearse a polished introduction and reading inside the six-minute maximum.
