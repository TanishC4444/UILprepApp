Contest overview

Know all three grades.

The test includes about 15 questions each from sixth-, seventh-, and eighth-grade Science TEKS plus five wildcard questions drawn from introductory biology, chemistry, physics, and scientific and engineering practices.

Sixth-grade TEKSSeventh-grade TEKSEighth-grade TEKSBio, chem, physics & SEPs
Scoring

Knowledge plus judgment.

Incorrect answers lose points, while skipped responses are neutral.

+5 for a correct response · −2 for an incorrect response · 0 for a skipped question.
Mastery plan

Skills to build before meet day.

  1. Review core curriculum across all three middle-school grade levels, not only the current grade.
  2. Build introductory familiarity with biology, chemistry, physics, and scientific practices.
  3. Reason from evidence, diagrams, and scientific models instead of relying only on recall.
  4. Use scoring-aware time management and skip low-confidence items when appropriate.
