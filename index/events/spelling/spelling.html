Contest overview

Every written detail counts.

Contestants write words dictated at roughly five per minute, then immediately complete a tiebreaker round. Grades 3/4 receive 50 words plus 15 tiebreakers; 5/6 receive 80 plus 20; 7/8 receive 110 plus 30.

Scoring

Precision is the entire score.

Capitalization, punctuation, omissions, and handwriting clarity all affect whether an entry is accepted.

Deduct one point for every misspelled word, including capitalization or punctuation errors.
Mastery plan

Skills to build before meet day.

  1. Know the annual UIL list deeply; it supplies roughly 80 percent of contest words.
  2. Study spelling patterns, roots, and etymology for off-list words.
  3. Practice dictation at the official pace with immediate tiebreaker rounds.
  4. Write clearly and apply capitalization, hyphenation, and apostrophes carefully.
